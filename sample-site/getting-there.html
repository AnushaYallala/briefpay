🗺️ Getting to Velugu Cove

It's easier to reach than you think — here's how to get to our little coastal paradise.

📍 Where is Velugu Cove?

Velugu Cove is a small coastal village on the eastern coast of Andhra Pradesh, India — roughly 45 km south of Visakhapatnam (Vizag). It sits along a quiet stretch of coastline between the Eastern Ghats and the Bay of Bengal.

The nearest major city is Visakhapatnam, which has an international airport, a major railway junction, and excellent road connections to Hyderabad, Chennai, and Kolkata.

✈️ By Air

✈️

Fly into Visakhapatnam (VTZ)

Visakhapatnam Airport (also called Vizag Airport) is the nearest airport with daily flights from Hyderabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai, and Kolkata. Airlines include IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, and Vistara.

🚗

Drive to Velugu Cove (~1.5 hours)

From the airport, take NH16 south towards Anakapalle, then follow coastal road signs for Velugu Cove. Pre-book a taxi through Lakshmi (₹1,200 one way) or use Ola/Uber from Vizag.

🚂 By Train

🚂

Visakhapatnam Junction (VSKP)

One of the busiest junctions on the East Coast line. Trains arrive from Hyderabad (Godavari Express, ~13 hrs), Chennai (Coromandel Express, ~12 hrs), Kolkata (Falaknuma Express, ~26 hrs), and many more cities.

🚌

Bus or Taxi to Velugu Cove

From the station, take an APSRTC bus towards Anakapalle/Yellamanchili (₹60, ~1.5 hrs) and ask for the Velugu Cove stop. Or call Lakshmi to arrange a pick-up taxi (₹1,000).

🚗 By Road

🛣️

From Visakhapatnam (45 km, ~1.5 hours)

Head south on NH16 past Anakapalle. At the Yellamanchili junction, turn east towards the coast and follow signs for Velugu Cove. The last 8 km is a scenic single-lane coastal road.

🗺️

From Hyderabad (~650 km, ~10 hours)

Take NH65 to Vijayawada, then NH16 north to Visakhapatnam, then follow local directions above. Overnight buses (APSRTC Garuda or private Volvo) run nightly from Hyderabad to Vizag.

💡 Travel Tips

  • 🔋 Mobile Signal — Jio and Airtel work well; BSNL is patchy. Download offline maps before the last stretch.
  • 💵 Cash — Carry cash. The nearest ATM is in Yellamanchili (8 km away). UPI works at Ravi's and most local shops.
  • 🌧️ Best Season — October to March (post-monsoon). Avoid mid-June to mid-September (heavy rains, rough seas).
  • 🛵 Local Transport — Rent a scooter from Lakshmi (₹300/day) or walk — Velugu Cove village is small enough to explore on foot.
  • 📞 Need Help? — Call Lakshmi at +91 98765 43210. She can arrange airport/station pick-ups.
