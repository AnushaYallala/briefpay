About the Homestay

Nestled just 50 metres from Velugu Cove's main beach, Sea Breeze Homestay is a family-run guesthouse that's been welcoming travellers for over a decade. Lakshmi takes pride in clean, comfortable rooms and a warm, home-cooked breakfast every morning — think idli, dosa, and fresh coconut chutney.

Whether you're here for a weekend retreat or a longer coastal escape, Sea Breeze is the perfect base to explore everything Velugu Cove has to offer.
