⛵ Cove Boat Tours

Guided by Meena and her crew — see Velugu Cove the way it was meant to be seen: from the water.

About Our Tours

Meena grew up on these waters. Her father was a fisherman, her grandfather before him. Now she runs Cove Boat Tours with a small, experienced crew who know every reef, every cove, and exactly where the dolphins play at dusk.

All tours depart from the Velugu Cove jetty. Life jackets are provided and mandatory. Meena's boats are well-maintained traditional wooden vessels with outboard motors — comfortable and safe.

🚤 Our Tours

🌅 Sunset Cruise

⏰ 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM  |  💰 ₹800/person  |  👥 Max 12 passengers

The most popular tour — glide along the coast as the sun paints the sky orange and pink. Includes chai and snacks on board. Perfect for couples and photographers.

🎣 Fishing Trip

⏰ 6:00 AM – 10:00 AM  |  💰 ₹1,200/person  |  👥 Max 8 passengers

Head out at dawn with the local fishermen. All gear provided — learn traditional line fishing, and if you catch something, Ravi will cook it for your lunch! (Ravi's kitchen charges apply.)

🏝️ Island Hopping

⏰ 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM  |  💰 ₹2,500/person  |  👥 Max 10 passengers

A full-day adventure visiting three tiny islands near Velugu Cove. Swim in hidden lagoons, explore tide pools, and enjoy a packed lunch on a deserted beach. Snorkelling gear included.

⚠️ Safety First

All passengers must wear life jackets at all times while on the boat. Children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult. Tours may be cancelled or rescheduled in case of rough seas or bad weather — Meena will notify you in advance. Your safety is our top priority.
